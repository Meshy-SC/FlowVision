# FlowVision Support — Meta Quest

FlowVision is a one-stroke line puzzle game for Meta Quest, published by Meshy.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

- **The goal:** trace every grey line of the figure in one unbroken stroke, each line exactly once.
- **Draw:** point at a dot, hold the trigger (or pinch) and drag along a grey line to the next dot. Your line always continues from the last dot it reached. Let go and pick up again from that same dot.
- **One-way lines:** a line with arrows can only be traced in the arrows' direction.
- **Dead ends:** if no untraced line leaves the dot you reached, the board resets so you can try another path.
- **Undo** takes back your last line; **Reset** clears every line of the level. There is no timer and no move limit.
- **Stars and score:** every solved level earns three stars and 10 points the first time.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb plays the level's solution, then leaves the order numbered on the dots so you can trace it yourself. It uses one solution credit the first time for a level; after that it's free for that level.
- Controllers and hands both work. Controller B or Y (Back) closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, each level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- If a purchase was interrupted, FlowVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
- Solution credits that you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in FlowVision for Apple devices stay there. Nothing transfers between Apple devices and Meta Quest.
- For a refund, follow Meta's refund policy in the Meta Horizon Store.

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your headset. If cloud backup is on in your headset's settings, Meta Horizon OS backs it up, and it comes back when you install FlowVision again.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
