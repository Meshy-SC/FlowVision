# FlowVision Support — Android XR

FlowVision is a one-stroke line puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

FlowVision opens as a window in your space. Look at a dot (or point with your hand or a controller), pinch, and drag along the lines.

- **The goal:** trace every grey line of the figure in one unbroken stroke, each line exactly once.
- **Draw:** pinch on a dot and drag along a grey line to the next dot. Your line always continues from the last dot it reached. Release your pinch and pick up again from that same dot.
- **One-way lines:** a line with arrows can only be traced in the arrows' direction.
- **Dead ends:** if no untraced line leaves the dot you reached, the board resets so you can try another path.
- **Undo** takes back your last line; **Reset** clears every line of the level. There is no timer and no move limit.
- **Stars and score:** every solved level earns three stars and 10 points the first time.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb plays the level's solution, then leaves the order numbered on the dots so you can trace it yourself. It uses one solution credit the first time for a level; after that it's free for that level.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, each level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, FlowVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in FlowVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install FlowVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
