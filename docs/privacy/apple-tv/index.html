Apple TV Privacy Policy

How FlowVision handles your information on Apple TV.

Last updated: March 19, 2026

Overview

FlowVision (“the App”) is a one-stroke puzzle game available on Apple TV (tvOS). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

Data Collection

FlowVision does not collect, store, or transmit any user data to our servers. We have no servers, no databases, and no access to your information.

We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any data whatsoever

Data Storage

All your data remains under your control:

  1. Locally on your Apple TV
  2. In your private iCloud account (if iCloud is enabled)

We have zero access to your data. Apple’s iCloud infrastructure ensures your data remains private and encrypted.

Third-Party Services

FlowVision does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising frameworks, or tracking services. The App uses only Apple first-party frameworks.

iCloud

If enabled, your data syncs via Apple’s iCloud across all your Apple devices, including Apple TV. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/privacy

Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell your data
  • Share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Track your behavior

Children’s Privacy

FlowVision does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Delete your data by removing the app from your Apple TV (local data) and disabling iCloud sync
  • Disable sync by turning off iCloud for the app in your Apple TV Settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
